How it works

Each card takes on the results of the meta decks that run it, then cards are ranked by the lower bound of a 90% Wilson confidence interval on win rate — so cards anchoring many strong, popular decks rise to the top. "55% · 6d" means a 55% win rate across 6 meta decks.
