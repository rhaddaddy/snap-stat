Blade

Blade is a Marvel Snap card that costs 1 energy and has 3 power. It has the effect: “On Reveal: Discard the rightmost card from your hand.”. It has 22 variants in the Pixel, Avengers, Luca Claretti, Boy-Thing, Dan Hipp, Baby, Combo Break, Fantasy, Nightforged, Midnight Suns, Peach Momoko, Mateus Manhanini, Stormbreakers, Alex Horley, Negative Space, Venomized, Envar Studio, Blade Legacy, Dark Gothic, Shadowborn, Dave Wilkins and Coran Kizer Stone categories.
