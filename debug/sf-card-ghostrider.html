Ghost Rider

Ghost Rider is a Marvel Snap card that costs 4 energy and has 5 power. It has the effect: “On Reveal: Bring back one of your discarded cards to this location.”. It has 24 variants in the Pixel, Robbie Reyes, Max Grecke, Damion Scott, Fantasy, Kim Jacinto, Baby, Knullified, Peach Momoko, Maria Wolf, Kael, InHyuk Lee, 1,000,000 BC, Björn, Nightforged, Hellfire Gala, PANDART STUDIO, Mateus Manhanini, Andrea Guardino, Andrew K Currey, Aaron Bartling, Champion, Shadowborn and Urban Aztec categories.
