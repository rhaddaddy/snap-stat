SNAP Stat terms and community rules

Last updated October 31, 2026

SNAP Stat is a free, unofficial fan app for MARVEL SNAP players, made by an independent fan. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Marvel, Second Dinner or Nuverse. By using it you agree to these terms and to the community rules below. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Using the app

  • It's free and comes as it is. SNAP Stat is offered without any promise that it will always work, be accurate or stay available. Stats, odds, forecasts and AI suggestions are estimates to help you, not guarantees.
  • Your account. Sign in only with your own Google (or Apple) account. You're responsible for what's posted from it.
  • Your AI keys. If you add your own AI keys, they stay on your device and you pay those AI services for what you use, under their terms.
  • The game's rules still apply. SNAP Stat only reads screenshots, files and screens you choose. Don't use it in any way that breaks MARVEL SNAP's own terms.
  • Don't misuse it. No attempts to break, overload or get around the app's security, read other players' private data, or pretend to be someone else.
  • Changes. The app updates itself, and features can change or end. If these terms change, the date above changes too.

Community rules (posts, profiles, decks, comments and alliance chat)

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate or slurs aimed at anyone, including about who they are.
  • Keep it clean. No sexual, violent or shocking content, and nothing involving minors that isn't plain game talk.
  • No spam or scams. No ads, selling accounts, cheats, phishing links or "free gold" offers.
  • Keep private things private. Don't post anyone's real name, address, phone number, email or account details, including your own.
  • Be honest. Don't fake results, impersonate other players or creators, or post other people's work as your own.
  • Names and pictures follow the same rules. Your handle, banner, profile picture, deck names and alliance name too.

Anyone can report a post with Report. The app owner and the social admins they choose can remove anything that breaks these rules, and can stop an account from posting. Alliance chat can be cleared by its writers, the app owner and the social admins.

Your content

You keep the rights to what you write and upload. By posting it in SNAP Stat you let the app show it to the players allowed to see it (see the privacy policy). You can delete your posts, comments, chat messages and your whole account in the app at any time.

Contact
