SNAP Stat privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

SNAP Stat is a free, unofficial fan app for MARVEL SNAP players, made by an independent fan. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Marvel, Second Dinner or Nuverse. This page explains what the app stores about you, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Google (or Apple), Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address and an account ID. Your email isn't shown to other players.
  • What you enter or import. Matches, decks, your collection and variants, wishlist, currencies, season and event progress, and settings. This includes anything you import from a SnapVault export.
  • Your profile and posts, if you share them. Your in-game name, country, alliance, profile picture and banner, stats summary (including this week's totals, a count of the cards you played and faced in tracked games over the last 30 days, and your results in tournaments you joined), featured deck, public decks, posts, comments, likes, follows and follow requests.
  • The stream overlay, if you turn it on. Your in-game name, rank, today's record and the live tracker's current match, readable by anyone with your overlay link until you turn it off.
  • Tournaments you create or join. The tournament name, its players' in-game names, the bracket and its results.
  • Reports. If you report a post, the report records who sent it, the post and the reason.

Everything is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), which Google runs for this app.

Who can see it

  • Only you: your matches, collection, wishlist, currencies, private decks and settings.
  • Other signed-in players: your public profile, public decks and posts. If you make your profile private, only followers you approve and your alliance see your stats, decks and posts.
  • The app owner: posts that players report, so they can be removed if they break the rules.

The app doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Things your device loads from other sites

  • Card art, card details, variants and locations come from Marvel Snap Zone (marvelsnapzone.com). Seasons, events, shop offers and balance history come from snap.fan. Your browser loads card pictures straight from those sites, so they see a normal web request from your device, as they would if you visited them.
  • Your snap.fan link, if you add one for automatic match import, is saved in your private account data. The app uses it only to read your own matches from snap.fan.
  • Screenshots you share or pick are sent to the AI service you set up so it can read them. SNAP Stat keeps what it logs, not the pictures.
  • Voice logging is optional. When you tap the microphone, your browser turns what you say into text using its own speech service (in Chrome, that's Google's). SNAP Stat only keeps the match it logs.
  • AI features are optional. If you add your own API keys (for Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT or Grok), they stay on your device, and your requests (like the cards in a deck you want improved, or a screenshot you choose to read) go to that company under your own account and their terms. If you set up the optional relay, requests for Perplexity and Grok pass through your own Cloudflare relay on the way.
  • Notifications are optional. When you turn them on, your wishlist and upcoming events are saved on your device so it can check for you. Nothing extra is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to More › Profile › Delete account. That removes your profile, posts, decks, matches, pictures and everything else saved to your account, and then the sign-in account itself. You can also erase just your data, or sign out, from the same page. Data saved only on your device (when you use the app without an account) is removed when you clear the site's data in your browser.

Children

SNAP Stat isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/rhaddaddy/snap-stat.
